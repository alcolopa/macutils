Three tools, one tiny app

Turn on only what you need.

Clipboard

Paste anything you copied

Hold ⌘⇧ and press V. A picker opens in the middle of your screen; keep tapping V to move through your history and let go to paste the one you want.

Caffeinate

Keep your Mac awake

Click the cup in the menu bar and choose how long: 15 minutes, a few hours, or until you turn it off. No extra app needed.

Monitor

Your Mac's vitals

CPU, memory, disk, network, temperatures and weather as menu bar widgets, with a drag-and-drop builder to arrange them your way.

Install in 3 steps

It takes about a minute.

  1. Download the zip using the button above, unzip it and drag MacUtils.app into /Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will probably warn that it "could not verify" the app. That's expected: MacUtils is open source and built on GitHub, but not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID.
  3. Allow it once. Run this in Terminal, then open the app normally: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacUtils.app

    Or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

The clipboard shortcut needs Accessibility access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility) so MacUtils can see ⌘⇧V and paste for you.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. MacUtils is free and open source. Donations are optional and help keep development going.

Does it store what I copy?

Only in memory, for the last 30 items. Nothing is written to disk, it is gone when you quit, and copies flagged as sensitive by password managers are skipped. You can clear it any time from Settings.

Why does the clipboard manager need Accessibility access?

macOS requires it for any app that listens for a global shortcut and sends a paste keystroke. MacUtils uses it for nothing else.

Why the security warning?

The app isn't notarized because that requires a paid Apple Developer account. You can read the code and build it yourself if you prefer.

How do I upgrade?

Quit the running MacUtils from its menu bar item, then replace the app in /Applications with the new version. You may need to grant Accessibility access again.

Which macOS versions work?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Clone the repo and run ./scripts/build_app.sh (requires Swift 6). See the README.
